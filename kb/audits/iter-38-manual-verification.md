## Contrast findings (from §B automated run)

The `npm run check:contrast` script (iter-38 §B) surfaced the following
WCAG AA failures on the iter-32 bordeaux palette. Per iter-38's "verify
only, don't adjust" rule, these are recorded here for **iter-38b** to fix.
Re-run `npm run check:contrast` after any palette change to confirm closure.

### Light (`:root`)

| Pair                                            | Ratio | Min | Status |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----- | --- | ------ |
| `--accent-foreground` on `--accent`             | 3.91  | 4.5 | FAIL   |
| `--sidebar-accent-foreground` on `--sidebar-accent` | 3.91 | 4.5 | FAIL   |

Both pairs use bordeaux primary (`oklch(0.5596 0.1431 32.4368)`) as the
foreground text colour over the muted warm-beige accent
(`oklch(0.9201 0.0259 48.4243)`). The contrast lift required to meet 4.5:1
either darkens the bordeaux foreground (changes `--primary` brand colour —
needs design sign-off) or darkens the accent background (cascades through
hover states across the app). iter-38b must pick a direction.

### Dark (`.dark`)

| Pair                                  | Ratio | Min | Status |
| ------------------------------------- | ----- | --- | ------ |
| `--muted-foreground` on `--muted`     | 1.91  | 4.5 | FAIL   |
| `ring on card` (`--ring` on `--card`) | 2.79  | 3.0 | FAIL   |

- `--muted-foreground` (`oklch(0.4526 0.0416 234.9214)`) is too dark
  against the dark-mode `--muted` (`oklch(0.2954 0.0422 266.7832)`). The
  carve-out in the design-system §1 note covers `--muted-foreground` on
  `--background`, *not* on `--muted` — this is a real fail.
- The focus ring (bordeaux) on dark-mode card backgrounds doesn't quite
  reach the WCAG 1.4.11 non-text 3:1 threshold. Lifting `--ring` lightness
  by ~0.05 OKLCH should close the gap without disturbing brand identity.
