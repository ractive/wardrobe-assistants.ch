# AGB / General Terms and Conditions — draft

This is a working draft of general terms for `wardrobe-assistants.ch` engagements. Not yet linked from the website. Send it as an attachment to quotes/contracts once a real legal entity is in place and a Swiss-qualified lawyer has reviewed it. Swiss CO (Obligationenrecht) is the default applicable law and Zürich is the agreed jurisdiction.

## 1. Scope

These General Terms and Conditions (Allgemeine Geschäftsbedingungen, "AGB") apply to all engagements between the operator (the "Crew") and the client (the "Production"). Any deviating terms apply only if confirmed in writing by the Crew.

## 2. Services

The Crew provides backstage wardrobe services (quick changes, ironing, repairs, wig and accessory tracking, load-in/load-out, laundry) at the dates, venues and crew sizes specified in the written quote accepted by the Production.

## 3. Booking and confirmation

A booking is binding once the Production has confirmed the quote in writing (email is sufficient). The Crew reserves the right to subcontract individual dates to qualified dressers under its responsibility.

## 4. Fees and payment

- Fees are quoted in Swiss francs (CHF) and exclude VAT unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- Travel, accommodation and per diems beyond the Zürich agglomeration are invoiced separately at cost or at the per-day rates listed in the quote.
- A deposit of 30% of the total contract value is due on confirmation. The balance is due within 14 days of the final invoice.
- Late payment incurs interest at 5% per annum from the due date, in line with Art. 104 CO.

## 5. Cancellation

If the Production cancels a confirmed engagement:

- More than 30 days before the first call: deposit is retained.
- 30 to 7 days before the first call: 50% of the agreed fee is due.
- Less than 7 days before the first call: 100% of the agreed fee is due.

Cancellations must be made in writing.

## 6. Changes to scope

Material changes to dates, venues, crew size or the services provided require a written change order. The Crew may adjust pricing in line with the new scope.

## 7. Liability

The Crew works to professional standards but accepts no liability for indirect or consequential losses. Direct liability is limited to the amount invoiced for the affected engagement, except where Swiss law mandates a higher standard (gross negligence, intent, personal injury).

The Production is responsible for ensuring that costumes and props provided are fit for the planned use. The Crew is not liable for fabric failure, dye-bleed, or damage caused by costumes that were not designed for stage use.

## 8. Insurance

The Crew carries professional indemnity insurance covering its own work. The Production is responsible for insurance covering its own costumes, props and personnel.

## 9. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delays or failures caused by events outside its reasonable control (illness, transport disruption, venue closure, public-health restrictions, acts of authority). The parties will use reasonable efforts to reschedule the affected dates without further charge.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, in particular casting information, unreleased material and rehearsal recordings. This obligation continues after the engagement ends.

## 11. Data protection

The Crew processes Production contact data and project information in line with the privacy policy at [https://wardrobe-assistants.ch/datenschutz](https://wardrobe-assistants.ch/datenschutz). Personal data of cast and crew shared with the Crew is processed only to deliver the service and is not retained beyond the engagement, subject to statutory retention obligations.

## 12. Applicable law and jurisdiction

These terms are governed by Swiss substantive law, excluding conflict-of-laws rules and the Vienna Sales Convention (CISG). The exclusive place of jurisdiction is Zürich.

## 13. Severability

If any clause is unenforceable, the remaining clauses remain in force. The unenforceable clause is replaced by a clause that achieves the same economic intent within the bounds of the law.
